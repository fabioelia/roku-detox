Let this page use your TV

Right now the TV only shares basic info. To see your channels and use the remote here:

  1. On the TV, press Home and open Settings.
  2. Go to System, then Advanced system settings, then Control by mobile apps, then Network access.
  3. Choose Default. Some TVs call it Enabled.

While this is on, any device on your Wi-Fi can control the TV. Set it back to Limited when you're done. It's the last step on this page.

        Your sites

        To add one, search the site in your browser and paste the address of its results page here. Roku Detox finds your search words in it and swaps in {q} (or put {q} there yourself). For example: https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query={q}

          Roku Detox reads only the text of each page, including the data many sites tuck inside it, so the site's ads, pop-ups and scripts never load, and videos from ad networks are skipped. For sites that only show results after their scripts run, the extension offers "Look closer": it opens the page in a hidden, muted tab for a few seconds (the site's own scripts run there, with Roku Detox's ad blocking), reads the results and closes it. YouTube results open in the YouTube app on the TV. Other videos play in Detox Home; videos locked by DRM can't be sent.

                    Detox Home on your TV

                    A calm home screen for the TV: a big clock, your favorite apps and inputs, and no ads. It also shows rule notices like "Time's up," messages you send, videos from web links, and an ad-free clock screensaver. Once it's installed it runs on its own: it gets your apps from the TV itself, so it never needs this computer.
