Roku Detox settings

Which TV

The toolbar button, the right-click menu, tv in the address bar and the keyboard shortcuts all use the TV you used last in Roku Detox. To always use one TV, type its address here.

Ways to use it

  • Address bar: type tv, a space, then a show, an app, one of your scenes, a button (pause, home, louder, off) or a link.
  • Right-click a video or a link: "Play this video on the TV". Select text: "Search the TV for …".
  • Toolbar button: send this tab's video, a small remote, your favorites and scenes, and a sleep timer.

Use it from your phone

Phones can use this extension through a small relay on the internet, while this browser is open. Everything passing through it is locked with your household's own key, so the relay can't read what you send or what's on your TV.

Open this link on your phone. Anyone with it can control the TV while this browser is open, so share it only with your household.

A new link stops every old one from working.

You set up the relay once with a free Cloudflare account; see relay/README.md in the Roku Detox folder.

Use the extension from a web page

The Roku Detox page can also run from http://localhost or http://127.0.0.1 and use this extension to reach the TV. It's off unless you turn it on, because any web app running on this computer could then work your TV and change rules.
